What your test plan should cover

  • Navigation: all links, menus, buttons go to the right place.
  • Interactivity: quizzes/forms/buttons behave correctly.
  • Compatibility: desktop + mobile, different browsers if possible.
  • Assets: images load, file sizes reasonable, no broken media.
  • Accessibility: readable text, contrast, keyboard use (where relevant).

How to present evidence

  • A test table: ID, test, expected result, actual result, screenshot, pass/fail.
  • Fixes: what you changed and why.
  • Retest evidence after each fix.
  • A short summary: what worked well, what needed improvement.

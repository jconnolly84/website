Task 3A(iv) Review effectiveness

Review the effectiveness of your website for the client and audience.

Effectiveness Client requirements Audience judgement
Try this structure:
Brief → Audience → Functionality → Assets → Improvements → Overall judgement.

What to review about the website

  • Does it meet the brief requirements (tick them off one by one)?
  • Is it easy to use (navigation, clarity, layout, consistency)?
  • Is the content appropriate and engaging for the audience?
  • Does the interactivity add value (not just decoration)?

What makes a strong evaluation

  • Balanced: strengths and weaknesses.
  • Evidence-based: testing results or user feedback.
  • Specific: name pages/features and describe impact.
  • Ends with a clear judgement: overall, it is / is not effective because…
